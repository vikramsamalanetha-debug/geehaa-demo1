Are you really open 24/7?

Yes. GeeHaw takes breakdown calls 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including nights, weekends and holidays. Call 731-225-9722.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Alamo and serve Jackson and the surrounding West Tennessee area, including Humboldt, Milan, Bells, Medina, Henderson, Lexington, Brownsville, Trenton and the I-40 and US-45 corridors.

Do you change semi truck tires on the roadside?

We work on Caterpillar, Cummins, Detroit and PACCAR heavy-duty diesel engines, plus aftertreatment systems (DPF, DEF/SCR, EGR).

Is a mobile mechanic cheaper than towing to a shop?

Often, yes. Many repairs can be done where the truck sits, which saves the tow bill and gets you back on the road sooner.
