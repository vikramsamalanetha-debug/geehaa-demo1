Aftertreatment problems are one of the most common reasons trucks lose power or go into limp mode. We find out whether the issue is the DPF, the DEF/SCR system, the EGR system or a sensor, so you don't pay for parts you don't need.

Aftertreatment services

  • Forced (parked) DPF regenerations
  • DPF and DOC diagnostics
  • DEF/SCR system faults, DEF quality and doser problems
  • EGR valve and EGR cooler issues
  • NOx, pressure and temperature sensor replacement
  • Derate and limp-mode troubleshooting

Why it matters

Ignoring aftertreatment codes can turn a simple sensor or regen into a clogged DPF or a damaged engine. Calling early usually means a cheaper, faster fix.

Common questions

Can you do a forced regen at my location?

In most cases, yes. We connect diagnostic software and run a parked regen on site when the truck is a good candidate for one.

Why is my truck in derate?

Common causes include a clogged DPF, DEF system faults, bad NOx sensors and EGR problems. A diagnostic check is the fastest way to know.

Do you work on aftertreatment for all engine brands?

We work on aftertreatment systems for Cat, Cummins, Detroit and PACCAR engines.
