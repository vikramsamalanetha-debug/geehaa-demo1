A tow to a dealer can cost more than the repair and take your truck off the road for days. As a mobile heavy-duty mechanic, we diagnose and repair your truck right where it sits, whether that's the shoulder of I-40, a truck stop, your shop yard or a job site.

Diesel repairs we do on the road

  • Check engine lights, fault codes and derates
  • No-start and hard-start problems
  • Fuel system, injectors and fuel filter issues
  • Turbo, charge air and boost leaks
  • Coolant leaks, hoses and overheating
  • Sensors, wiring and electrical troubleshooting
  • Air system and air leak repair
  • Belts, alternators and starters

Engines we work on

We work on the heavy-duty diesels you see every day in West Tennessee, including Caterpillar (C7, C13, C15), Cummins (ISC 8.3L, ISX, X15), Detroit (DD13, DD15, Series 60) and PACCAR (MX-11, MX-13). We use computer diagnostics, including Cummins INSITE, to find the real problem before replacing parts.

Who we help

Owner-operators, small fleets, dump truck and logging outfits, farms, and construction companies across Jackson and Madison County.
